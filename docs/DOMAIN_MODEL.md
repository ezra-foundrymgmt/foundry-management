# Domain model

- **Prospect** — acquisition relationship, pipeline stage, activities, owner, and explainable Foundry fit score. Conversion retains this record.
- **Application** — structured inbound submission and review state, optionally linked to a prospect and eventual creator.
- **Creator** — canonical identity, ownership/security status, commercial relationship, assignments, and health.
- **Content** — pillars, franchises, requests, safe metadata, social posts, and creator-relative performance.
- **Experiment** — hypothesis, control/variant, primary metric, confidence, result, and scale/iterate/hold/retire decision.
- **Report** — daily, weekly, or monthly structured operating assessment with data quality, anomalies, bottleneck, and evidence-backed actions.
- **Workflow** — versioned definition, durable run, observable steps, retries, blockers, and idempotent provisioned resources.
- **Integration** — organization- or creator-scoped connection state and health; credentials remain in secure provider storage.
- **Task** — internal accountable work tied to a creator, prospect, report, or workflow. Creator deliverables are separate.
- **P&L** — receipts, Foundry commission revenue, direct fulfillment cost, contribution profit, and margin.
- **Health** — explainable weighted score with a hard critical security/compliance override.
- **Incident** — owned operational, platform, relationship, data, integration, security, or compliance issue.
